This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our Telegram bot and related services.

When banking services are used, banking information is accessed only for the purpose of verifying transactions and processing deposits. We do not request or store your online banking password.

4. Use of Information

Information is used to operate the service, verify deposits, maintain user accounts, prevent duplicate transactions and provide customer support.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain transaction records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Your Rights

You may contact us regarding your personal information and request access, correction or deletion where applicable under relevant law.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the service administrator.
